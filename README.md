# __Cosmic-Evolution-26S__
### Objective: Analyze emission line strengths and line widths to identify signatures of star formation and potential active galactic nuclei, in order to explore how black hole activity varies across different stages of early galaxy evolution, and the effects this has on star formation in these early galaxies.
